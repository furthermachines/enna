# Code of conduct

Enna is meant to become a standard that people from many organizations can work on together. That only works if everyone can take part without being treated badly. This code applies to everyone who takes part in the project's spaces: its public repository and that repository's issues, and any discussion, meeting or channel the project runs.

## What we expect

- Treat people with respect, especially when you disagree with them.
- Argue about ideas, with evidence. Do not attack the person who holds them.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- Credit other people's work, and say plainly when a claim is a guess.
- Accept that a decision has been made, once it has, even when it is not the one you wanted; the decision records say how to propose a change.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation or threats, in public or in private.
- Insults, slurs, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background or abilities.
- Sexual language or attention that is unwelcome.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussion, or pushing a commercial interest at the expense of the work.

## Reporting and enforcement

The project's owner is responsible for enforcing this code until there are other maintainers.

**No channel for conduct reports exists yet.** They will go to an email address, which the owner has not chosen yet (`GOVERNANCE.md`, decision 3). When it exists, this file will name it. Security problems have their own private channel (`SECURITY.md`).

Reports will be handled in confidence. Responses are proportionate: a private conversation, a public correction, a temporary restriction, or exclusion from the project's spaces for serious or repeated harm. Anyone who reports a problem in good faith will not be penalized for it.

This code is written for this project. It will be reviewed when the project has more than one maintainer.
