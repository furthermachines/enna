# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in Enna, the Business Agent Protocol.

## Issues

Issues are welcome, for questions and for proposals. A good issue:

- names the section it is about, such as 9.7, or the conformance kit's case format;
- quotes the text;
- proposes a change, or says what is unclear.

The draft's owner decides what goes into it ([`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md)), and an issue is the way to propose a change.

## Pull requests

Pull requests are not merged here, for two reasons:

- this repository is regenerated from the project's build at each release;
- whether contributors to the specification text make a patent commitment is undecided ([`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md), "How governance could evolve"). Its license, CC BY 4.0, gives none ([`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md)).

## Security

Security problems go through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or by email to <security@businessagentprotocol.org>, as [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) says, never a public issue.

## Conduct

Everyone who takes part follows [`CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Report a conduct problem by email, to <conduct@businessagentprotocol.org>.
